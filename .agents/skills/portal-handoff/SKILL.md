Read the assigned Issue and newest comments before changing status or code.
Use docs/team-plan.md to resolve owner and reviewer; do not assign yourself
another participant’s files without coordination. Link PR to the Issue.
Report result, branch/commit, exact checks and outcome, limitations and next step.
Keep architectural decisions in docs/ instead of only a comment.
Do not equate an Approve review with a participant confirming their local clone.
Do not close a backend task while real Auth/REST acceptance is still pending.
Publication authority comes from the user request and AGENTS.md; a skill does
not grant permission to post messages or deploy. Chats do not share inboxes.
